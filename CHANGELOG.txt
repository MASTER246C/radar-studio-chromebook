Radar Studio 0.2 • 10 October 2026

Vitals continuity fix
- Vitals no longer disappear when ordinary tracking frames omit a vitals TLV.
- Every decoded frame in a USB chunk is processed for vitals, even when its last
  frame contains only tracking data.
- Last valid estimates and up to 150 actual received waveform samples are held
  separately for each subject. Sparse frames never replace them with zeros.
- Each subject shows the receipt time, source frame number and age of its vitals.
  Live vitals older than five seconds are labeled STALE, even if tracking frames
  are still arriving. Offline, replay and synthetic values are labeled separately.
- No interpolation or missing-sample estimation is performed. New acquisition
  sessions clear retained histories; switching presentation does not.
- Existing cards/canvases are reused instead of removed/recreated every frame.

Five seconds is a display freshness threshold, not a medical validity criterion
or claim about the firmware's measurement cadence. This fixes a confirmed UI
state bug; it does not establish that the hardware/USB stream never drops data.

3D view and track trajectories
- One interactive sensor-coordinate 3D view replaces the separate top/front
  plots. Drag to orbit, wheel/pinch or +/- to zoom; Top and Front remain presets.
- X/Y/Z axes, sensor origin, view bounds, and Reset help orient the scene.
- Optional per-track trajectories have 5/10/30/60-second arrival-time windows
  and a Clear control. Synthetic demo data also leaves visible trails.
- Trails split after a missing target, skipped frame numbers, a >2-second
  reception gap, or session reset. IDs are firmware track IDs, not verified
  people identities, and reuse without an observable gap cannot be identified.
- Trails cover only what this one radar reports in its view, not a whole-house
  map. Reception timestamps are not synchronized measurement timestamps;
  accelerated replay uses playback arrival time, not original capture timing.

Version 0.1 — initial prototype
- Dependency-free browser UI, separate CLI/data Web Serial connections, explicit
  configuration send with acknowledgements, point/track top and front views,
  experimental vitals, synthetic demonstration and local raw-byte replay.
- Project owner reported live operation on their Chromebook.
- Known sparse-vitals display limitation addressed in v0.2; no broad hardware
  validation or medical accuracy claim.

Public release packaging
- Added beginner launch/troubleshooting and developer handoff documentation.
- Vendor firmware/configs, private manifests and recordings omitted.
- No open-source license selected.
