CONFIGURATION GUIDE

This source release intentionally excludes vendor configurations and firmware.
Use the exact configuration already known to work with the firmware currently
installed on your own board. Obtain vendor software from its official source
and follow its license. Do not switch or flash firmware just to try this viewer.

Load your local .cfg through the viewer. Loading sends nothing. Sending is a
separate explicit action, and commands can stop, reconfigure and restart the
radar. Review the commands and CLI replies. Stop on timeout or firmware Error.
Presentation selection changes the display only, not firmware configuration.

Coordinates are raw SENSOR coordinates (X lateral, Y forward, Z up). The viewer
does not apply the original TI sensor-position or mounting-angle transforms.
Configuration filenames do not establish coverage, accuracy or compatibility.
Do not select executable firmware .bin files as recorded-data replay files.
