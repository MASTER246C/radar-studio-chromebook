Camera label review

Radar Studio · Workflow and limits

Stop recording and confirm radar idle before reviewing. Any browser-readable video can be reviewed. The local pose model supplies suggestions for one visible person; its activity rules are experimental. Human review, camera/radar alignment and eligible radar measurements are required for training.

1. Open a video segment

No video selected. Nothing is uploaded.

Analysis samples five frames per second, at most two minutes per pass. Long recordings are stored in separate video segments. MediaPipe Pose Landmarker Lite detects body landmarks; Radar Studio's separate rules suggest sitting, standing or walking. Unknown and ambiguous observations stay unknown. Review requires WebAssembly, a worker, and browser video decoding.

Ready to open a local video.

2. Review the suggestions

Play the video, correct each label and its start/end, then check its Reviewed box. Suggestions stay in the exported record alongside your decisions. Unchecked decisions cannot train the radar model.

3. Bind the recording and match the clocks

This step is required for a radar training export. Import the original session and video receipts from the recording folder. Select timing files that cover this video segment to choose the correct radar run.

Recording not bound.

Identify at least three events observed in both the video and radar, spanning the reviewed interval. Enter one event per line: video seconds, radar sensor timestamp in microseconds, uncertainty in milliseconds. Host arrival times alone do not establish alignment. Only the interval covered by the anchors is eligible.

Alignment unmeasured. Review can be saved, but labels cannot yet be mapped to radar.

4. Save the review

Save this beside the original video and receipts. The recorder project's camera import command verifies the original files and produces the existing radar dataset format. It retains measurement quality exclusions and uses only explicitly reviewed labels with a valid alignment. Instructions
